## 4. What may be claimed

- **Claimable.** With the database snapshot, three of four J1 judges agree with the verifier at F1 ≥ 0.884, and
  gemma at 0.990. Without it, every judge either abstains heavily (coverage 0.43–0.59) or, in Aya's case, accepts
  70% of failed executions. Aya is the Stage-1 judge family and the policy's family.
- **Not claimable.** Judge accuracy against humans. "Consensus is truth": the judges disagree on a third of cases,
  and the verifier remains authoritative. That aggregation beats the best judge: it does not.
- **Temperature 0 is not deterministic** at the router: repeat agreement is 0.934–0.990.
