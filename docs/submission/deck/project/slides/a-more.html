APPENDIX A5 · EARLIER REGISTERED STUDIES AND WHAT WE DO NOT CLAIM

Context, and the limits of the claim

Reward information (A12, ID1). A database reward makes read-back the default in 5 of 5 seeds; an acknowledgement reward of identical form never does. From a warm start that never reads, nothing changes: the reward selects checking, it does not create it.

Judges (J1). With a database snapshot the best LLM judge reaches 0.989, a majority committee 0.897: judges are not verifier-grade.

Companion theory paper. Group normalisation under a shared hidden state (TH1, AD-C1); a separate claim, paper/main_v3.pdf.

Not claimed

a new RL optimizer · a novel benchmark design · safety or generalisation beyond the stated splits · real-system validation · an independent verifier · a mechanistic circuit
