# PREREGISTRATION J2: Aya Expanse baseline judge, order and repeat robustness, error-calibrated aggregation, double annotation

Registered 2026-11-24 (IST), before any J2 judge output. Registration time and hashes: `results/j2_hashes.json`
(`python scripts/lab/freeze_j2.py`). J2 extends J1 (`PREREGISTRATION_J1.md`, unchanged); J1's outputs are read, never
rewritten. P2's committee run (`committee_p2.py`) is the lead engineer's and is not duplicated here.

## 1. Questions

1. How does Aya Expanse 32B, the Stage-1 judge family, compare with the four-family J1 committee on the same cases and
   views? (Historical baseline. It is the same model family as the policy, Cohere Aya, so self-preference bias is
   possible; it is therefore reported separately and enters aggregation only in a labelled second variant.)
2. Are the judges' Q1 answers stable when the answer options are shown in reverse order, and when the identical
   prompt is sent again at temperature 0?
3. Does error-calibrated weighting (fit on the calibration split against the verifier) beat a simple majority?
4. What is needed for human double annotation and adjudication, and what do the labels show once they exist?

The SQLite verifier remains ground truth for whether the database writes took effect. No judge output enters any
reward or ActionProof metric.

## 4. Human calibration (blocked on a second person)

- The author labels the 76 stratified calibration cases in `label.html` → `data/human_labels.json` (J1 §5).
- A **second annotator** labels the same 76 cases in `label_b.html` (different order, own storage, export
  `data/human_labels_b.json`), blind to the author's labels, the verifier and the judges.
- `build_double_annotation.py` then writes `adjudicate.html` with only the disagreements; the two annotators decide
  together → `data/human_labels_adjudicated.json`.
- Inter-annotator Cohen's κ per question is reported. Judge metrics against humans use the adjudicated labels when
  they exist, otherwise the author's labels, labelled "single annotator".
- No label is ever generated by a model.

## 5. Analysis (`analyze_j2.py`, frozen with this file) → `results/j2/j2_summary.json`

Per judge and view, main set against the verifier: coverage, selective accuracy, precision, recall, F1, Cohen's κ
(Q1 vs goal_met; abstention counted as negative), false acceptance (says took effect when it did not), false rejection
(1 − recall), Brier score and ECE (5 bins) from the stated confidence, Q3 vs false_success, Q4 vs unrefunded duplicate,
Q2 vs report status, Q5 vs read-back (a proxy, labelled as such), and every case answered against the verifier with
confidence ≥ 0.9 ("confident contradictions"). Also: share fooled on unverifiable failures (J-H1 strata, transcript
view); order-reversal and repeat agreement and κ on Q1; Aya paraphrase agreement; disagreement share and Fleiss κ;
error-calibrated naive-Bayes aggregation (per-judge P(answer | goal_met) with add-one smoothing and the class prior,
fitted on calibration, evaluated on main) vs majority (ties abstain), each for the four J1 judges and for all five.

**Predictions (descriptive; no "supported" rule):** Aya is fooled on unverifiable transcript-only failures at a share
≥ 0.25; repeat agreement ≥ 0.95 for every judge; order-reversal agreement lower than repeat agreement; weighted
aggregation has F1 ≥ majority on transcript_state.
