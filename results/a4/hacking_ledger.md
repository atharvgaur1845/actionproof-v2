## The hole we found in our own state reward

Unrecoverable refunds (G = 0 in every episode), mean reward by behaviour over all logged episodes:

| behaviour | episodes | PCSTR-d | A7 state arm (noreport) |
|---|---|---|---|
| continue, report completed | 357 | -0.133 | +0.167 |
| stop, report completed | 187 | -0.300 | +0.000 |
| stop, report failed | 406 | +0.000 | +0.000 |

The A7 state arm (`pcstr_d_noreport`, no report terms) pays "keep writing, report completed" +0.167 over stopping honestly, through partial credit for the steps that did land. PCSTR-d's report term reverses the order. This is why the state arm's honest-failure rate on unrecoverable refunds is not a claim we make.
